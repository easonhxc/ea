# UniPath — concise feature overview

UniPath is a persistent university-planning system: AI imports a student's profile; AP/IB/A-level courses are evaluated subject-by-subject against the intended major; projects, outputs and interdisciplinary fit feed a bounded hybrid admission model; the app builds a 20-school U.S. Common App list with T30/T35/T50 filters plus separate U.S./UK tracks and 2026 ranking context; it automatically plans ED/EA/RD/UC/UCAS rounds and runs Monte Carlo simulations; it matches summer programs, research and competitions while generating original projects from the student's gaps; it creates a graduation-year-aware roadmap, keeps a continuous AI advisor/history, supports school preferences and verified high-school context, bilingual settings, admin data overrides and non-admin demo accounts.
