# UniPath v2.3 — List calibration, rounds, course-level academics and project-content fit

## Automatic ED / EA / RD
The user does not need to save colleges before seeing a round strategy. `predict()` first builds the 20-school U.S. Common App list, then `buildAutomaticStrategy()` assigns the recommended early route, one ED I when appropriate, a conditional ED II fallback, compatible EA/EA2 options and RD core. Each model-plan school now carries a short `round_reason`.

## Exactly 20 U.S. Common App recommendations
The model separates U.S. Common App, U.S. outside-Common-App systems, UK and other countries. The Common App track is exactly 20 with reach/target/likely quotas and profile-band guardrails. It cannot be filled by UK universities.

## Course-by-course AP / IB / A-level model
Detailed courses take precedence over aggregate counts. AP score, IB HL/SL + subject score, A-level subject + grade, and whether a course is completed/current/planned are evaluated against field-specific prerequisites. Planned courses provide only weak evidence.

## Project-content + interdisciplinarity
Activities are not scored from category labels alone. The model reads names, descriptions, roles and outputs to identify technical and bridge dimensions. High-interdisciplinarity fields require both a technical core and the relevant humanities/social/environment/service/design bridge.

## Recommendation coherence
Ultra-selective schools have stricter profile-band admission into the recommendation pool. Rankings never rescue a school into the list. U.S. and UK tracks have separate display bands so a UK realistic option is not presented on the same ladder as a U.S. lottery school.

## 2026 rankings snapshot
Every university in the built-in catalog has a `rankings-2026.json` row. QS 2026 and U.S. News 2026/2026–27 are display-only. `NR` means not ranked / not verified in the current snapshot; it is preferable to inventing a number.

## Final v2.3 hardening
- Calculus now also satisfies the broader mathematics foundation; IB/A-level Mathematics can partially satisfy a calculus requirement. This removes the previous double-penalty where AP Calculus BC could score highly yet still leave `mathematics` as missing.
- Explicit school preference has more weight in personalized list ordering, but only after probability/profile guardrails. Prestige or ranking cannot rescue an implausible option.
- A binding ED recommendation cannot be generated just because a school is a high-probability Likely. Without an explicit 9.5/10 preference, ED candidates must be Lottery/Super Reach/Reach/Target schools with an actual modeled fit.
- UK bands remain separate from the U.S. Common App ladder; no UK school can fill or calibrate a U.S. Common App slot.
